Motion Desk

Where the framework itself goes up for deliberation.

Motions are formal proposals about how the Compass reads: the tenets, the rules that govern calibration, the modifiers, and the moral framework beneath all of it. A work may be cited as evidence inside a motion, but a work is never the subject of one. The room is open to read; filing opens with shared sign-in.
