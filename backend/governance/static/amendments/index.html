Amendments

The public record of changes proposed to the tenets.

The tenets are publicly amendable, not by vote but by deliberation, and the burden of changing them is deliberately high. Proposals are argued on the Motion Desk, in the open. The Compass keeps the record. What follows is every proposal that has been raised, where it stands, and what became of it.
