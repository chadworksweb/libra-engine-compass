How the Compass Changed Its Mind

The law maturing, one ruling at a time.

The compass does not change its mind about a song. It changes its mind about how to read. Each entry below is a rule of construction or a reading lens the compass adopted, with the case that forced it. This is the public record of the law evolving, kept by the instrument that owns it. The per-song readings live with each product; this is the shared standard beneath them.
