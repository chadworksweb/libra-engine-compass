# Inhabited Voice Tenets - Recalibration Lens

Ratified 2026-06-21. The earnest sibling of the satire lens. Loaded into the calibration
engine ONLY when an admin invokes an inhabited-voice recalibration on a song; it does not
replace the standard rubric - it sits alongside it as a parallel reading lens. Apply the
standard rubric first, then re-read through this lens.

Validation bench (a change to these tenets that breaks any of these is a regression):
- RESCUE (lens lifts out of Degraded): "Choose Lit", "Dark Nights", "All I Wanna Do" (Chad
  Lewine); "Government Hooker" (Lady Gaga).
- HOLD (lens must NOT lift; they stay Degraded): "Life is a Ride", "'Til I Pop", "Grab A Pack"
  (Chad Lewine).
- CEILING split: "Dark Nights" / "Choose Lit" carry compassion or a named path -> low Elevated;
  "FAT LIP" / "All I Wanna Do" / "Government Hooker" witness with no exit -> Decent.

The inhabited-voice recalibration exists for the legitimate edge case the satire lens cannot
reach: a narrator who EARNESTLY inhabits a degraded, fallen, numbed, or suffering state in order
to PORTRAY or WITNESS it truthfully - to hold up a mirror - rather than to endorse, model,
glamorize, or invite it. Satire exposes through IRONY (inversion, excess, mockery, a frame-break
wink). The inhabited voice exposes through EARNEST FIDELITY plus an in-lyric TURN: the song stops
performing the state and reflects on it. There is no wink; the truth-telling is the point.

## What Stays the Same

- **Words only.** Author identity is irrelevant. Character voice is irrelevant. The compass reads
  what the words on the page DO, not who is saying them. Knowing an artist would never endorse the
  state may TIP YOU OFF to look for the turn, but it is never evidence and can never substitute for
  the turn being in the words. Inhabiting a voice is, by itself, never a reason to move a calibration.
- **The full rubric still applies.** Five tiers, contamination model, dominant-arc method,
  start-at-zero, route and two-axis discipline - all unchanged.
- **Cite the lyrics.** Every move from the literal read must cite the specific lines that turn and
  the lines that carry the cost. "It feels like he is just portraying it" is not a case.

## The Bright Line vs Satire

- Exposure by IRONY - inversion, excess past plausibility, mockery, a frame-breaking wink -> the
  **satire** lens.
- Exposure by EARNEST FIDELITY plus an in-lyric turn from inhabiting to reflecting -> the
  **inhabited voice** lens.
- A song can carry both; read each where it operates and weight by the dominant arc. "This Is A
  Raid" stays satire (it winks and breaks frame ironically). "Government Hooker" is inhabited voice
  (earnest persona of subjugation, the ache on the page, a protest turn at the end).

## What Changes

When inhabited voice is the verified hypothesis, you re-read the same words asking: **does this
inhabitation WITNESS the state, or ENDORSE it?** Not what the artist meant; what the words DO.

If "endorses" - even with the lens applied - the calibration does not move; the literal reading
stands. If "witnesses" - the tier may shift upward to reflect what the words are actually doing.

## The Inhabited Voice Tenets

### IV1. The turn is the gate. (load-bearing)

The song must, IN THE WORDS - typically the chorus, the hook, or the ending - TURN from inhabiting
the state to reflecting on or commenting on it: naming the cost, questioning it, protesting it,
stepping back, or pointing past it. The turn need not resolve - a reflective or questioning hook
with no exit still satisfies the gate (it simply lands lower under the ceiling, IV5). But absent a
turn somewhere in the words, the inhabited-voice reading does NOT hold and the literal calibration
stands, no matter how much ache is present. Examples of the turn: "I only wrote this song to show
you what's goin' on"; "next time, light, I'll choose it"; "the drugs got us making bad decisions,
nobody listens 'til we die"; "stop f***ing me government hooker." The turn is what separates a
mirror from a performance.

### IV2. The ache is the support-tell.

The visible COST corroborates the turn. Emptiness, pain, loss, entrapment, or damage shows through
the depiction. A state rendered as hollow, aching, or trapped is being witnessed; the same state
rendered as triumph, pleasure, conquest, or flex is being endorsed. The turn plus the ache is the
witness; a turn with no ache anywhere is a weak reading - look again before lifting.

### IV3. No glamour, no invitation.

Witness shows; it does not seduce or recruit. If the lyrics glamorize the state, frame it as
aspirational or cool, or invite the listener to come along ("let's", "come with me", "grab a
pack", "ride with me"), the inhabitation tips back toward endorsement and the gate is not met.

### IV4. Truth over seduction - read what the detail is FOR.

Concrete detail that serves RECOGNITION of the damage reads as witness; the same register of
detail that serves ENTICEMENT to the high or the conquest reads as endorsement. Decode what the
specifics do: make the wound legible, or make the behavior attractive.

### IV5. Observation isn't action - the ceiling.

Shared with satire (S4). The turn earns a song OUT of the degraded tier because it is not
endorsement, but witness is observational, not constructive. Placement:
- Witness with NO exit (an honest mirror that names or questions the trap and stays in it) ->
  DECENT, near neutral. It told the truth; it built nothing. ("FAT LIP", "All I Wanna Do",
  "Government Hooker".)
- Witness PLUS compassion, solidarity, or a named path out of the state -> LOW ELEVATED.
  ("Dark Nights" carries "I'm here for you through the dark night"; "Choose Lit" turns to "next
  time, light, I'll choose it".)
- Witness PLUS enacted internal work - the narrator actually processing and arriving somewhere
  new - can climb higher Elevated. The inhabited-voice credit alone does not get there.
- Almost never Ascended: that articulates universal truth or enacts collective healing directly,
  which is construction, not portrayal.

### IV6. Failed witness is endorsement - DEFAULT TO ENDORSEMENT.

The main guard against abuse. With no ironic structure to point to, this lens is the easiest to
misuse ("he is only portraying it"). So the burden runs the other way: if there is no turn in the
words, if the cost is not on the page, if the rendering glamorizes or invites, or if a listener
taking the words at face value could reasonably hear straight celebration, the reading does NOT
hold and the literal calibration stands. When unsure, the literal read wins.

### IV7. The contamination model still applies.

An inhabited-witness song can still be contaminated: if the dominant arc witnesses but specific
lines cross over to glamorize, recommend, or invite the very thing they portray, flag those lines
as contamination. The tier may move up under the lens; the discrete endorsing artifact is tracked
separately and does not disappear.

### IV8. Inhabited mode and witness mode - read them separately, then weight.

Songs move between INHABITED mode (living the state in-voice on the page) and WITNESS mode (the
turn: cost surfacing, a step-back, a protest, a thread of compassion or longing). Identify each
section; the witness mode is the evidence the gate is met (IV1). Weight by the dominant arc: the
witness mode must be strong enough to carry the reading. If it is too thin - the song mostly leaves
the listener inside the state with no turn - the lens does not hold (IV6).

## How to Run an Inhabited-Voice Recalibration

1. Read the lyrics top to bottom under the standard rubric; note where it lands literally.
2. Write down the literal charge_summary - your reference point.
3. **Find the turn (IV1).** Locate the specific lines, in the chorus/hook/ending, where the song
   stops inhabiting and starts reflecting/commenting/questioning/protesting. If there is none, STOP:
   the literal calibration stands.
4. Run the ENDORSEMENT TEST: with the turn in hand, does the whole arc WITNESS (turn present, ache
   visible, no glamour, no invitation) or ENDORSE (no turn, glamour, recruitment)? Cite the lines.
5. Identify INHABITED-mode vs WITNESS-mode sections (IV8).
6. If the reading holds, apply the ceiling (IV5): no exit -> Decent; compassion/path -> low Elevated;
   enacted internal work -> higher.
7. Write the new charge_summary in the standard voice - what the song IS doing under the lens, not
   what its surface depicts.
8. Record: original literal tier, the cited turn, the endorsement test, the mode breakdown, the new
   tier, the ceiling consideration.

## Output Format

Standard calibration JSON, preceded by these reasoning fields in this order:

```
LITERAL_SUMMARY: [The charge_summary under the standard rubric without this lens - the starting point.]
TURN_TEST: [The gate (IV1). Quote the specific chorus/hook/ending lines where the song turns from inhabiting to reflecting/commenting/questioning/protesting. If there is no turn in the words, write "No turn - inhabited-voice reading does not hold" and return the literal calibration.]
ENDORSEMENT_TEST: [With the turn in hand: does the arc WITNESS (turn + ache + no glamour + no invitation) or ENDORSE? Cite the lines that settle it.]
MODE_BREAKDOWN: [Which sections are INHABITED mode and which are WITNESS mode? Quote the lines that mark each.]
CEILING_CHECK: [Apply IV5. No exit (Decent), compassion/path (low Elevated), or enacted internal work (higher)? Not Ascended by witness alone.]
```

These fields force the discipline of the lens rather than a vague "he is just portraying it so it
is fine." Each is reviewed by the admin alongside the proposed calibration.
